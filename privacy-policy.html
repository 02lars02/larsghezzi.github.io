Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Applications may store information locally on your device so that the game or utility can function. This local information can include:

  • Game progress and save data, such as completed levels, career or simulation progress, scores, achievements, unlocks, inventory, statistics, and other gameplay state.
  • Preferences and settings, such as sound settings, notification preferences, language or display choices, tutorial status, and other configuration values.
  • Purchase-related status, such as whether a premium feature or entitlement has been unlocked, when needed to provide purchased functionality.
  • Local gameplay or diagnostic counters used by some Applications to operate features or help understand game state. Unless a third-party service is explicitly used, this information remains on the device.

Depending on the Application, local information may be stored using platform preferences, local databases, or files in the Application's storage area. Lars Ghezzi does not operate a general user-account system or a developer-operated cloud database for normal game progress in the current Applications covered by this policy.

Information Processed Through the Application

When a third-party service is enabled, the Application or that service may process technical or usage information needed to provide its function. Depending on the Application, platform, consent choices, and service configuration, this can include:

  • Device and other identifiers, including an advertising identifier or similar identifier where available, permitted, and required for advertising or measurement.
  • Internet and network information, including Internet Protocol (IP) address, connection status, and related network information needed for online services.
  • App activity and advertising interaction information, such as ad impressions, clicks, interactions, session-related information, and events necessary to provide or measure advertising.
  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system version, language, app version, and general technical information.
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information where collected by a platform or enabled service to maintain stability, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, or improve service operation.
  • Purchase information, such as the product purchased, transaction status, purchase receipt or token, and entitlement status, as processed by Apple App Store or Google Play purchase systems.
  • Game-service information in Applications that use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, such as sign-in status, platform game account information made available by the service, achievements, and leaderboard scores.

This information may be used to provide and maintain the Applications, save or restore functionality, complete purchases, deliver advertising, measure advertising, provide leaderboards or achievements, prevent fraud or abuse, troubleshoot technical issues, maintain security, and improve the Applications.

Location Information

The current Applications covered by this policy do not intentionally request precise GPS location for their normal functionality. Third-party services, including advertising or platform services, may derive an approximate location from an IP address or similar network/device information according to their own policies. If a future Application or feature requires precise location, it will request the relevant device permission and the applicable store disclosure will be updated before that location is used.

Advertising and Tracking

Some or all ad-supported versions of the Applications use Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, device information, approximate location inferred from network information, and other information described in Google's policies to provide, limit, protect, measure, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, age-related settings, consent choices, device settings, and the configuration of the Application. Where consent is required by law, the relevant consent or platform mechanism should be presented before processing that requires consent.

On Apple platforms, if an Application or advertising partner engages in tracking as defined by Apple, the Application uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before accessing the device's advertising identifier or engaging in tracking that requires permission. You can allow or deny this request and later change it under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying tracking permission does not prevent access to the Application's core functionality, although ads may be less personalized and some advertising measurement may be limited.

In-App Purchases

The Applications may offer in-app purchases or premium upgrades. Purchases are processed by the platform from which you obtained the Application, such as the Apple App Store or Google Play. Lars Ghezzi does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account information. The Application may receive purchase-related information from the platform, such as a product identifier, transaction or purchase status, receipt or purchase token, and entitlement information, so that purchased features can be delivered or restored.

Apple and Google process purchase and payment information according to their own privacy policies, account settings, store terms, and legal obligations.

Notifications

The Applications may use local notifications for features such as reminders, timers, events, or return-to-game prompts. Local notifications are scheduled by the Application on your device. The current Applications covered by this policy do not rely on a Lars Ghezzi-operated remote push-notification server for their standard local notification functionality. If remote push notifications are added in a future version, the related disclosure will be updated as appropriate.
