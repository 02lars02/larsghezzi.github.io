4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Applications may store information locally so that features work correctly. This can include:

  • Game and activity progress, such as completed levels, simulation or career progress, scores, achievements, unlocks, inventory, statistics, session state, and other gameplay information.
  • Preferences and settings, such as sound settings, notification preferences, language or display choices, tutorial state, consent-related settings where applicable, and other configuration values.
  • Purchase entitlement state, such as whether a premium feature or ad-removal entitlement has been unlocked.
  • Local counters and diagnostic state used to operate features, manage gameplay, or troubleshoot local app behavior.

This information may be stored in preferences, local databases, or files in the Application's storage area. I do not operate a general user-account system or developer-operated cloud database for normal game saves in the current Applications covered by this policy.

Some or all ad-supported versions of the Applications use Google AdMob. AdMob and participating advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP address, device information, advertising interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, and related information to serve, protect, limit, measure, and where permitted personalize advertising.

Whether advertising is personalized depends on factors such as your region, age-related settings, device settings, consent choices, and the Application's advertising configuration.

Where applicable law or advertising-platform rules require consent before particular advertising-related processing, the required consent choice must be obtained before that processing occurs. You may be given controls to accept, reject, or manage advertising choices, and those choices may affect whether personalized, non-personalized, contextual, or limited ads are shown.

Apple App Tracking Transparency

On Apple platforms, if an Application or advertising partner engages in tracking as defined by Apple, the Application uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the device's advertising identifier or engaging in tracking that requires permission. You can allow or deny this request and later change your choice in the privacy settings provided by iOS or iPadOS.

Denying tracking permission does not block the Application's core gameplay. Advertising may still be displayed, but it may be less personalized and some advertising measurement may be limited.
